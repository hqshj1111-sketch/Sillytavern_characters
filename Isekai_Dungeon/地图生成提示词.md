# 地图图片生成提示词

## 通用美术风格（所有图片适用）

日式 RPG 游戏风格（JRPG 地图），手绘质感，柔和渐变色块地形，清晰标记，温暖自然的色调。类似勇者斗恶龙、最终幻想系列的世界/区域地图风格。无 UI 元素、无连线、无网格。

- **视角**：正鸟瞰俯视（top-down bird's eye）
- **比例**：1:1 正方形
- **内容**：海洋环绕中央大陆，大陆主体 5 个国家，南部海域群岛
- **标注**：国家名称写在对应区域上

**提示词：**
> A Japanese RPG style world map, top-down bird's eye view, 1:1 square ratio. A single large fantasy continent surrounded by ocean. The continent is divided into five distinct regions with natural geographical transitions between them:
> - **Central-north region**: A massive volcanic mountain range running north-south, with dark gray/black volcanic peaks, lava veins, snow-capped summits, and deep rift cracks. Label: "龙脊联邦"
> - **Northern region**: Snow-covered rolling hills and dense dark green taiga pine forests, with white frozen lakes and pale tundra coastline. Label: "霜境王国"
> - **Western region**: Dense dark green giant forest covering the entire western landmass, with deeper shadowy areas in the south. Tree canopy texture visible from above. Label: "密林双廷"
> - **Eastern region**: Vast golden-green temperate grasslands and plains, crossed horizontally by a wide blue river winding from the mountains to the eastern coast. Label: "原野同盟"
> - **Southern region**: Warm orange and tan desert, with scattered emerald-green oases, rocky canyon textures, and ancient ruin dots. Label: "金沙帝国"
> - **Southern ocean**: Below the continent, a chain of tropical islands with coral reef rings visible in turquoise shallow waters, surrounded by deep blue ocean. Label: "潮汐联邦"
> Clean, readable Chinese text labels placed directly on each region. No grid lines, no UI elements, no connecting lines. Hand-drawn JRPG map aesthetic with soft color gradients.
